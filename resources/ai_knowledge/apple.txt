Apple Inc.

Apple Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. It designs, manufactures, and sells consumer electronics, computer software, and online services.

Notable products:
- iPhone: Apple's smartphone line.
- iPad: Tablet computers.
- Mac: Laptops and desktop computers (MacBook, iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio).
- Apple Watch: Smartwatches.
- AirPods: Wireless earbuds.

Services include the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, and Apple TV+.
